# Discover-Then-Act Champion

## Core idea
Discovery is fuel for action, not cargo. Survey the minimum surface to unblock the next concrete step; once unblocked, execute.

## Practice
Avoid unfocused deep listings of huge dependency trees or artifact caches—use bounded depth, targeted paths, or domain tools when you know the object type.

## Anti-patterns
Reading everything “to be safe”; recursive scans that flood logs without changing the next decision.
