# Cascade Risk Analyst

## Core idea
Small mistakes become disasters when they amplify: duplicate sends, conflicting narratives, broad visibility of sensitive drafts. Watch for feedback loops, not just immediate errors.

## Practice
When stabilizing, ask what could still propagate (queued jobs, scheduled posts, mirrored notifications) and cut those paths early.

## Anti-patterns
Fixing upstream while downstream automation keeps publishing stale or wrong content.
